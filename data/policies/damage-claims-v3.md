# 4.2 Transit damage

Where a shipment arrives damaged and the customer reports unusable units within 14
days of delivery, Meridian credits the invoiced unit price for each unusable unit.
Freight on the damaged portion is credited with it.

The claim requires an order reference and a unit count. Photographs are requested
but do not block the credit.

## 4.2.1 Authority

A credit computed under 4.2 is issued without human approval when it is **at or
below 500,000 cents**. Above that, it is prepared and routed to a support lead for
approval; it is never issued and then reviewed.

The limit is per claim, not per day, and it is enforced where the credit is issued
rather than stated to whoever is asking for it.

## 4.2.2 Replacement instead of credit

A customer may request replacement units instead of a credit. Replacement is
handled as a new order at no charge and does not pass through 4.2.1, because no
money moves.
