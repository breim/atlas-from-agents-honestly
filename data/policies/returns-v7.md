# 7.3 Returns of electrical components

## 7.3.1 General window

Electrical components in unopened original packaging may be returned within 90 days
of delivery for full credit.

## 7.3.2 Opened packaging

Opened electrical components are returnable within **30 days** of delivery provided
no unit from the shipment has been installed, energised, or field-modified. This
applies to the RB-400 relay line and to every part in category EC-2.

A return under 7.3.2 is credited at the invoiced unit price. Restocking is waived
for gold-tier accounts and charged at 15% otherwise.

Installation is determined by the customer's declaration on the return
authorisation. Meridian does not require physical inspection before issuing the
credit for claims at or below the standing authority limit.
