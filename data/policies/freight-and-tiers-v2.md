# 2.1 Expedited freight by contract tier

| Tier | Standard freight | Expedited freight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Gold | Covered | Covered |
| Silver | Covered | Charged at cost |
| Bronze | Charged at cost | Charged at cost |

Coverage follows the tier recorded on the account's active contract at the time the
order ships, not at the time it was placed. A tier change mid-quarter does not
re-rate shipments already dispatched.

Expedited freight on a replacement issued under damage claims 4.2.2 is covered at
every tier, because the original shipment failed.
