# 9.1 Relay warranty

The RB-420 relay carries a 36-month warranty from date of delivery covering failure
under rated load. The RB-400 carries 24 months on the same terms. The RB-380 is end
of life and carries the balance of its original 24 months with no extension.

Warranty is void where a unit was operated outside its rated envelope, and a
warranty claim is not a return: it is assessed under this section, never under
returns 7.3.
