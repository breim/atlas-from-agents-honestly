# 7.3 Returns of electrical components

Electrical components may be returned within 60 days of delivery in unopened
original packaging.

**Opened electrical components are not returnable.** Where the packaging seal has
been broken, the sale is final regardless of whether a unit was installed. Claims
for opened relays, including the RB-400 line, are to be declined with reference to
this section.

Exceptions require written approval from the regional sales director.
